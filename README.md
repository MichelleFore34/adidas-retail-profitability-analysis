# Retail Channel and Partner Profitability Analysis

**Question:** Should Adidas drop a retail partner or sales channel to reach a 5% profit target?

## Key findings
- Channel mattered far more than retailer: in-store operating margin was 36%, outlet was 46%
- No single-retailer exit reaches the 5% target in a 30% volume reallocation model
- Found and corrected a 10x Units Sold error in 40% of rows by cross-checking sales, margin, and profit

## Approach
1. Cleaned 9,648 transactions (2020-2021 US sales data)
2. Compared operating margin across 6 retailers and 3 channels
3. Tested differences with ANOVA and t-tests
4. Modeled a 30% volume reallocation
